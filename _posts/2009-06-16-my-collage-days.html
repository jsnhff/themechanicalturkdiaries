Hi I’m Mal. I love my collage days because of cultural programs. The same thing i hate too, because i can’t attend my classes. I use to be in stage always. A special notice will go to our class teacher’s for permission. We are six girls important for collage cultural dances.Our collage is new to our city, trough this cultural programs only it got famous. So always our collage gives importance to dance. Especially our state cultural dance is the specialty. Its really interesting and harder dance too. I use to avoid this dance first but my collage V.P won’t allow me. By compulsion i continued this good dance. We stayed in 2nd price when compared to all other elder collages. This was really great to us. I was selected in my collage for an inter collage competition. It was a group dance. All other people come from other collages but we belong to same university. This was my unforgettable remembrance in my life. I got lot of stress on this dance practice. But at last we are the 1st in our country.I’m so proud of it to share with you all.Thank you.
