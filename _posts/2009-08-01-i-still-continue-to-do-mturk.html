I graduated in 1999 from a technological school earning my Associates Degree in Accounting Computer Administration. Going out in the workforce, I used all the Accounting I was taught and applied it to the accounting tasks at hand and gained a lot of experience and over 10yrs worth of experience. I was looking for something legit to do online to supplement my income and I found MTurk.com I love Mturk.com because you can complete the tasks on your own time at your own pace and get paid for it. Just over two months ago, I moved up to Wisconsin from Virginia and have continued working in the Accounting field. I still continue to do Mturk tasks and I recommend this to anyone that wants to legitimately make some extra cash.
