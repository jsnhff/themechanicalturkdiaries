I am a college student who recently found Mechanical Turk. So far I have really enjoyed my experience working on this website. I enjoy being able to work from my home and most of the HITs are really easy to complete. With the recent state of the economy, it is pretty difficult to find a job, so this is definitely a great opportunity. You will not find a better and easier way to make some extra cash, and we all know college students need that the most. I live in a pretty large college community and I do not know anyone else who has used Mechanical Turk. I will definitely be recommending it to all of my family and friends. The best thing about Mechanical Turk, is that you do not have to have qualifications for the tasks. The tasks are simple and do not take much time at all. Overall joining Mechanical Turk is one of the best decisions I have made.
