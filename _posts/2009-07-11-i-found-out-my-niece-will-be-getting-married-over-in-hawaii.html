I got started turking here recently when I found out my niece will be getting married over in Hawaii. What I figured I would do is to do this thing for the next two years. She will be getting married in two years. I figured that if I did this part time I could actually make enough to get over there and to enjoy myself. I have figured that I would need to do about $3.00 worth a day to fulfill this in due time. I have really enjoyed it a lot it has opened up a lot of avenues in the past. I keep telling everyone that I am literally saving pennies up to go to Hawaii. They are not thinking that this work’s out. If and when you can get a few that pay something worthwhile it is worth the effort and time to do this job. I had heard about this from several sources of information and thought what the heck. I would love to give it a try. I gave it a try and I am gaining pennies and dimes each and every day. The longer I do this I know I am getting closer and closer to my goal in the future. There are day’s when I don’t feel like it. But I do it anyways. If I can keep the average at about $3.00 a day I am okay with that. Which really that doesn’t take that much time and effort to let that add up the way it does. Turking is a way to make some extra cash when I needed it the most.
