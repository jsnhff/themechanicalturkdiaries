Another day on the Turk. It seems like its getting harder to get any hits. I guess with the economy being what it is, more and more people are scrapping for even the smallest pieces of change. So, I answer a couple of questions, screen a few images, and take a few polls. Still, I cant seem to get to my quota for the day. Will my obsession have to go unfilled today, or will I have to spend the entire day trying? Wait! There’s a new hit looking up record release dates at five cents a pop. Maybe if I can get a few of those, I can get on with my life for the rest of the day. Mostly Latin music, with a couple of old country hits. I’m getting bored pretty quickly. It looks like its going to be a long day after all. Just as I’m about to give up hope, there’s a hit that asks me to tell my story. Seems kind of crazy to me, after all if I had an interesting story to tell I’d probably not be wasting my life turking for peanuts. Nonetheless, I give it a shot, hoping that maybe someone will find this little glimpse of the turking world to be some interest. I think I hear my real life calling, so I better get back to it….
