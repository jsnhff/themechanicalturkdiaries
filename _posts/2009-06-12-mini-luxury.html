Mechanical Turk has been a huge impact on my life. From leading a life of a daily routine of going to college and coming back straight home without being able to have some fun as lack of money was the basic reason to living a life of a mini luxury at the level of a student. Here, luxury refers to those extra activities that we students can do such as watching movies, hanging out coffee shops etc. And how did I manage to afford this extra luxury? Answer is Mturk! The site that allows you to earn money for doing easy jobs such as writing articles and reviews and verifying sites and taking up surveys ! It’s such an amazing concept of providing work and a living to those who need it and providing a 24x7 large scale globally spread labour for companies or individuals. I am indeed very grateful to my buddy who introduced me to the site. Initially, I did not figure out the easier way of earning. I used to click hits which payed0.05$ which was quite a small amount and this way I had to work on 20 hits to just earn a single dollar. But soon I found out hits that pay 0.20-0.30$ and which were quite easy to work on. And in a month, I had earned around 40$! Well, that might be a small amount for most of the people. But it was more than sufficient for me to meet my daily requirements. Well, Mturk is indeed a great site.
