I have been a Mechanical Turker since last month. I had looked into it before, but never had enough time to do it and make it worthwhile. Now I am out of school and looking for a full-time job, and it is great to be able to make a little money from home. I like the freedom of being able to choose the jobs I do and pick how much or how little I will work every day. The tasks don’t always pay much, but it pays for coffee and movies and other little things that I might not otherwise have money in my budget to buy. Most of them only pay a few cents, but even if I only earn a dollar or two a day, in the end it adds up and some tasks even offer bonus payments for good work. The income is a nice supplement to my savings, and I enjoy being able to pick from a variety of tasks. The tasks can be visual, audio, or writing, so I rarely get bored doing all the different kinds of tasks on the site. Since most of them are short, and don’t require any special knowledge, this site is ideal for people like me, who don’t always have long periods of time available to be on the computer, or who like the freedom to be able to work at odd hours. I’m glad I found this site and have time to earn a few dollars a day.
