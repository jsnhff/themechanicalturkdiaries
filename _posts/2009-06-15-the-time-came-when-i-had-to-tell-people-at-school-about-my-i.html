The time came when I had to tell people at school about my immigration. First I told another one of my friends Bob. He knows how its feels when one of your friends move to another country because my godfathers wife was her nanny. She moved to England last year and because me and Bobwere very close to her it hurt. We still talk to her on the phone but its not the same. I know how it feels aswell because my best friends was Kip and he moved to Melbourne and it’s worse because I don’t speak to him any more. Anyway back to telling people at school. Most of my guy friends laughed and thought it was a joke or something. I soon convinced them I was not kidding. I think they felt a bit sad, but I was not as close to them as my friends that are girls and plus girls are much more mature and sensitive! My teacher was also sad when I told her. Although my life and lifestyle will be better I cant help but to think about the people I am leaving…that’s why I got their addresses and phone numbers! That way I can stay in touch with my friends and family!
