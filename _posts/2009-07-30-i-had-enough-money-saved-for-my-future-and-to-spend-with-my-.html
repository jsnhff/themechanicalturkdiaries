I am a Machinical Turker and have been doing online job with mturk for six months as a full time job, I have to say it is really great. I have been able to earn enough money to decorate my home and furnish my home too. I had enough money saved for my future and to spend with my friends too, but because I had earned money through Mturk, I was able to use it for my PR visa to australia. I came to know about this mturk through my friend, and thought I would check it out and see what all the fuss about it was. I was hooked right from the start. I started with fairly simple tasks and as my confidence grew, I started accepting more involved HITS. I’m not saying you will ever get rich by doing HITS, but it’s a great way to use your time till you could get one good job and still contiune it as a part time job (instead of watching TV or mindlessly surfing the web), and earn some extra pocket money while doing it. This earnings form mturk is really getting bigger and bigger day by day and very useful to me and my family. I dined in inexpensive restaurants. There have been times when I’ve been a little low on cash and have used my earnings to pay my phone bill or my electricity bill That’s what I love about Mturk and really hoping that i would continue this for ever and make some useful pocket money to me and my family.
