I am the youngest one in my family. Being a civil engineer most of the time I would spend at construction sites. I am a diplomo holder and I am working for the past 12 years. I started my career at a very early age. I joined as a trainee and now I am a Quality Manager. I was working in Gulf for around two years and very recently got married. Registered with mturk to utilize my leisure hours usefully. I love to watch movies and fiddle with my computer. As soon as I come home from office I will start my work on my laptop. My wife will keep scolding me for this. I was actually very irresponsible when I was a bachelor. I was a spendthrift. I had passion towards electronic appliances. Which ever comes new in the market I will be in a mood to try it out.But now, after I got married I scarificed my passion. Because I need to lead a budget life and it is tough for me to enjoy these extra stuff. I have so many targets/annual goals set for this year. Planning to buy a plot at my hometown and construct a 3 storey cost effective apartment. I would be the sole engineer of my apartment. Need to buy a luxury car too. Not sure whether I would be able to make my dream true but it’s my strong belief that only dreams can make you reach higher and higher places. I am expecting a baby within next couple of months and feeling so proud about it. Infact after marraige I became more responsible and started thinking more about my future. Became very family concerned and leading a happy life.
