“I’m bored, I’m bored, I’m bored.” These three words last year kept going through my mind after facing retirement. So what was I suppose to do to pass my time and earn a little money on the side? I got to tell you, I got pretty sick of going down the road looking for bottles and cans. Bit of a trick, it was, avoiding being hit by cars. I really just wanted to stay home and try to find something other than surveys to do. Hey, I found something interesting…Amazon Turk. Now to see if it’s a scam. It wasn’t! The first time I withdrew earnings from them, it was sitting right there in my bank account two days later. I got addicted to the darn thing. It took a while to find my niche and learn how to looks up hits in the many way’s they have, but I did it! Sometimes I’d be lucky if I made a dollar a day, other times it would be several dollars a day. I’d sit at my computer on cold winter nights when you couldn’t get out of the driveway and pass my time and just watch the money grow. I’d get really excited when I did an article and earned six dollars for an hour worth of work. Amazon Turk has helped me buy things I normally wouldn’t spend the money on. I was able to buy a small freezer using the most of the money I had in my account. Love ya, Turk! In one year I’ve made $650.00!
