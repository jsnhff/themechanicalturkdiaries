I just recently started working on Mechanical Turk. I have been enjoying myself so far, especially seeing the small amounts of change add up to real money. It is actually interesting and fun to look up some of the requested information. I consider myself to be a Google expert so it is not difficult to find the answers. Writing is not my strong suit but I am trying to get more comfortable writing so that I can make even more money on Mechanical Turk. The writing assignments seem to pay the most money and there are many of them to be found. I have been without a job for nearly a year and Mechanical Turk is helping me get through this difficult period of time. Mechanical Turk allows me to earn a bit of money and not be so hard on myself for being without a job for so long. This economy has been very difficult on me and my family. I am scrimping and saving every where I can, cutting coupons and shopping sales. Mechanical Turk is allowing me to actually make money. I do not plan on making a living off of this but it is something for now. I will probably continue to use Mechanical Turk once I get a job just to have a little bit of extra money on the side. Everyone needs more money than they have. Plus I enjoy doing the simple tasks while watching television or listening to music. I am very glad Mechanical Turk is here in my time of need.
