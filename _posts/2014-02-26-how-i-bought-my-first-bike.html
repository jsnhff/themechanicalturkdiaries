How I Bought My First Bike:

It was 2 years ago , this same day , I joined mturk.I was not really into it fully.But later I found that I could really make money out of it and it has given me a confidence.As I have recently joined college I do not have enough time to make money from other part time jobs.One of my friends told me about Mturk.After joining, I made 11$ on my first day and started to make 2 dollars daily as I can spare only about 1 hour after my college time.All the hits that I do mainly consisted of simple surveys and took only minutes to complete.Two years after that fateful day today im here telling the world how happy I am to get my first bike and that too out of my own pocket.I made 4500 dollars in the first year and withdrew it to pay my tuition fee.I recently withdrew about 1800 dollars and got my first bike.Without mturk I don’t think I would have had both the confidence and money to get myself a bike without having to borrow from my parents.
