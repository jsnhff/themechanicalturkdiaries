Unemployed student and mom who is desperate for work, that’s my byline. Thanks to an awesome website dedicated to helping people find legitimate online work without being ripped off I am a Turk. I find myself spending long hours looking for enough hits so I can afford a tank of gas but the well paying hits a few and far between. I need to pay my late car note and get a tank of gas, rent a textbook for the summer session and by bread. (sigh) Gosh can’t these pending hits turn over a little faster? I work hard, fast, and thoroughly but it’s not enough. This hard working Turk would be super excited if I could get a call back or even interview from anyone. Minimum wage would be awesome…did I just say awesome about a minimum wage job, I did? See what this economy is doing to me. Wow I’m wasting time aren’t I? I better stop ‘cause a hit is awaiting and I need that .20 for ten minutes of my time which only averages out to .02 cents a minute and $1.20 an hour (sigh again) I’ll do it anyway because the alternative is that I won’t have twenty cents to put in my overdrawn account. I hope Amazon appreciate their Turk’s because we so are not getting any love in our inbox. No thanks for remotely accessing the sweatshop at a cost the higher than I can afford. Oh well back to Turking, Turking, Turking day and night. Signed Your Anonymous Turk in L.A.
