I am an enthusiastic youth, searching for online jobs to raise my daily allowance for college needs. I was disappointed with lack of honest online paying jobs and loads of scam sites. Then one day, my friend introduced me to Amazons Mechanical Turk, which changed things for me. It has been such a great experience working on a wide variety of tasks and getting decent pay for it. Mechanical Turk always provided with good no of feasible tasks and also the approval rate and the time taken to approve has been excellent. I started off with an aim of earning at least 1$ per day. But by the end of the first month, I had earned 45+ $ which was a result of my dedication to the site. Later on, though my earning rate decreased, it has still motivated me to continuously search for good tasks and work on them. This extra money that I earned through the site helped me meet my additional needs of college life such as clothes, movies and eating out at restaurants which was earlier only an aspiration. Now with my success on this site, even friends and relatives have got inspired to try out this site. Not only does this extra money fulfil my fancy needs but also it gives me a sense of satisfaction and contentment that I can spend on my additional needs without having to ask the cash from my parents. I would really love to thank the team behind the site to come up with such noble idea of helping people in need of jobs as well as helping those in need of talented labor.
