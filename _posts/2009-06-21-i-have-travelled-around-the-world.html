I have travelled around the world and have been with different individuals, groups, societies and cultures. I have found out that we are divided in many aspects of life. The only thing that could probably unite humanity in its humblest sense is love which is the catalyst towards understanding. For when one loves, understanding seems to be more present than those who does not love nor care about others. And when there is understanding, is there anything left to question? Problems arises, because there are questions which are not answered. But understanding it all whether it is known or unknown, relives anthing or anyone the need to be answered. Understanding results in the acceptance of all things. When one learns to accept all things, it comes back again to understanding, which in turn, will again results into love. And the cycle continues like all things that is eternal. Therefore, love begets understanding, and understanding begets acceptance and then going backwards, acceptance results to understanding, then understanding results into love. This is the basic need of human relationships and with these, the world may end up a happy world after all. This is the eternal cycle of life. Where all things begin, and all things end but the end is the start of the beginning…
