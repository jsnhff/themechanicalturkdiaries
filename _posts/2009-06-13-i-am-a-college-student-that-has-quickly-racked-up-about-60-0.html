I am a college student that has quickly racked up about $60,000 of debt, with at least 2 years go to before I leap into law school! I believe that higher education is to pursue a higher level of knowledge – not to lead me to a career. Though I do want to go to law school, chances are my life pursuits will lead me elsewhere first. I have a thirst to immerse myself in every culture of the world. Unfortunately, that cannot be done for free. I am at home living with my parents for the summer, trying to make money. I am a cashier at a local grocery store, but that only gives me 10 or 15 hours each week. I also picked up a babysitting job that is all day, twice a week. I have been searching for other jobs, but like everyone else in the U.S. right now, I am coming up short. This has led me to online services, such as kgb_ and mechanical turks. The pay isn’t too great for either, but since I have nothing else to fill my time, it is better to be making some money than no money at all. In the rest of my time, I like to call myself a musical explorer. I spend a lot of time listening to new music, just to broaden my horizon with new styles. I also spend a lot of time with my adorable nephew, as well as garden and enjoy the sunshine while it’s here for a few months!
