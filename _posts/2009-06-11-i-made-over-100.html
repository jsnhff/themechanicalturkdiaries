This is just something fun to do when I come home from work and I don’t want to do alot of thinking. I like to find the ones that are multiple choice, surveys,the easy ones. Granted you can do ones for alot more money, but I fell they require to much time then there is a good chance you can be rejected. Quick and easy that’s what I say. It’s too bad they don’t pay more,because I think more people would do them. Some of my friends think I’m nuts for spending so much time for so little money, but once again, I’m a hundred dollar richer than I was in November. The one nice thing about doing the hits is that with some of them you can get a bonus, that’s always exciting to see. Evey Sunday I get on the e-mail to see how much money I made for the week, they send you a weekly report and a monthy report. When your ready to cash in they put YOUR money right in to your bank account, easy access. It a beautiful thing!
