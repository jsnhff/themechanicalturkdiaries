It’s been a great week on the Turk. The American Astronomical Society dumped thousands of students tests out here looking for us to transcribe student comments at nine cents a pop. I made my daily quota several times over on these. Then Amazon Requester put out a ton of five cents hits that only required searching for release dates for various musical releases. I did a bunch of these, to fill out my quota foor most of the rest of the week. If only it could be that easy every day.
