I am an unemployed college student from Washington trying to scrape up a little cash. I have tried like mad to get a job for over six months but haven’t had any luck except for a few random one of two time gigs. I hoped to be able to use Mechanical Turk to make a decent amount of money, but the well paying hits seem to be few and far between. I often spend hours on the computer every day so I figured I might as well be doing something is remotely productive. I haven’t made too much so far, but every little bit helps right now. Things are looking up for the summer, and I hope to get a full time over the summer so I can make more money and pay for school in the fall, but I will still use Mechanical Turk to get a little extra cash. When I go back to school in the fall I will still use it then too, even if I have a part time job. If I don’t get a job over the summer, I will probably use Mechanical Turk more, though I know it will not provide me with enough money to get by. However, some money I better than none. If I could find more HITs that paid better, I would use Mechanical Turk even more, and I know many other people would as well.
