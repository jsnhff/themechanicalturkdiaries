I started doing Mturk a couple of years ago. I was making jack shit and ended up with a bunch of rejections so I gave up for a while. I came back every so often but the few pennies I was making was not worth it. Fast forward to December 2010 and I am very pregnant with two kids already, my husband had lost his job and we were struggling. I logged on to MTurk again in hope of…well something. This time I did research and found the best ways to make money. I make on average $3.00 a day/30.00 a week now. I was able to have gas money and buy things for the baby, who is due any day now. I am really glad I came back to Mturk.
