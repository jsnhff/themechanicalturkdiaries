I find myself in my late 40’s in front of a keyboard. I loved the computer before the world become unable to function without them. I remember the 300 baud modem and actually plugging the telephone headset into the modem box. Telephones had cords. My printer was just a really fast typewriter, actually striking the letters onto the page. I stay home to educate my children. Not because I am a fanatic, but because I really love watching the learning process takes place. The “aha” moments in life get fewer as we age, but in the young, they are magical and I don’t want to give them to someone else. I rescue dogs and find them good homes after nursing them back to health and restoring their faith in mankind. I used to do stand up comedy, but it gets harder and harder for me to come up with new material - not that there is less to laugh at but because the industry as a whole has become so crass. I am a woman of faith and hope in Jesus. I find comfort in thinking that all the things that happen here aren’t all that there is. I look forward to a time when there is peace, true peace on this earth. I love raspberry sherbet. I bring the receipt back into the store if I am undercharged. My hair is short now because it looks like a cross between a bad Seuss drawing and a brillo pad.
