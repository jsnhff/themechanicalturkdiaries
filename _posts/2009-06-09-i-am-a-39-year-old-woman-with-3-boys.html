I am a 39 year old woman with 3 boys, who are my best blessing and the cause of much heartache. I wish I knew all the right answers when I was asked a question. I have constant strugles like anyone else trying to juggle kids, work and a social life. I went into the Marines right after high school graduation. I love to learn new things and want to live life to the fullest. My dream of traveling outside of the US is on my list of accomplishments I hope to acheive soon. I also want to learn an instrument and get back in shape. I love to read and watch movies. I am also lazy and lose interest in things very quickly, I am known to start something and work on it for awhile and then just go on to something else and never finish what I started. Currently I have a started quilt, a bag of yarn and needles, the makings of many a craft project all just hanging out to be finished at a later date. I am a good friend and daughter, a pretty good sister and struggle every day to be the best Mom I can be. I feel lucky and blessed to be where I am, with good health, family and friends.
