I currently plan to be a sucessful forensic scientist and I plan to be able to support myself especially with a little help from turking. Everyday I have the support of my family and my dog which is very comforting in these tough times. I still like to travel a lot, I love seeing new places and broadening my horizons. When I travel I try to be a bit more outgoing because I meet so many new people but once I get back home, I’m back to my usual person, who plans to be shy in their comfort zone. After all, what more can I do to cause more changes? Everyday for me is work, surveys, turking, taking care of chores and the occasional relaxation but my daily life schedule remains the same each day. You would think that I lived the same day every single day because there is little difference between the days.
