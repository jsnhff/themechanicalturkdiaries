I join the Mechanical Turk 4 month’s ago. And in this short period I got a great amount of money. Sometimes, I get the work which is of my interest, I feel great in doing this type of work. My brother told my about this, firstly I thought that the work would be very hard and I can’t be able to it .But when I register myself in Mechanical Turk and open it, then I found that I has work for all, means in every field. But sometimes I feel that after searching long I can’t get the hit for me, and if get so it’s reward amount is very less. Searching hits take more time than doing it. But still it’s good to work with mechanical Turk. Before this I have done lots of online jobs, but there is always risk of dumpling up your reward amount. Some of the companies do not pay for your work or some will pay less than the amount decided earlier. But mechanical turk give full reward for your work as told earlier before submitting the hit. The best thing about it is that you can do any other work also along with mechanical turk. In September its our anniversary and I am planning to give a surprise gift to my hubby, for which I need a great amount of money. I hope I could get so many appropriate hit so that I can purchase gift for him.
