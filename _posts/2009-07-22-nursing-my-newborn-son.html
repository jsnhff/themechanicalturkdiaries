As a new mom, I spend a considerable amount of time nursing my newborn son. At first, it was enough for me to watch him during my new 8-hour a day job feeding him. However, I soon found that I could read books and do other things like internet research while he was feeding. I became very good at typing with one hand while I was looking up what age he might start holding his head up, etc. Then, I realized you could make some money while doing internet research and I found Amazon Mechanical Turk and here I am! Since I am a math teacher, I will be going back to work in the fall. I plan on using my experience as a mechanical turk in my classroom. It will be a great story for a lesson on estimation…
