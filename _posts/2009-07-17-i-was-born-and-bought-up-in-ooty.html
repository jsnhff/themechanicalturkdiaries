I was born and bought up in Ooty. A small hill station in India. But a famous tourist spot. I was not much matured still I completed my 12th standard. The only things I knew is my parents and playing with toys. Because of no quality education in Ooty my father joined me in a college in Coimbatore. It is 100 kms from Ooty. There first time I fell in love with a girl. But she refused. I started smoking, then drinking, and then I started bunking my classes. But something said she will one day love u. I did all these things by seeing Tamil movies only. Then in my second year of college life I was dismissed due to lack of attendance. Then I moved back to Ooty and lived there for more than a year. Then her thoughts again took me back to Coimbatore. When I was back to Coimbatore she had completed her degree. Then some how I managed to change her mind and we were together for 5 years. I have spent about 4, 00,000 which of some I borrowed and some I worked hard to earn. For her and have wasted my youth age also. At last she went married without even informing me. Still I live in her thoughts only.
