I really enjoy doing the hits on Mechanical Turk. Like a lot of people nation wide the economy has had a negative impact on me and my family. I have looked for other odd jobs to supplement my income and have searched a lot for online opportunities. There are a few legitimate work at home things but Mechanical Turk is, in my opinion the easiest to use and has the best variety of tasks to do. It gives me a great way to earn an extra few dollars every week! (Every dollar adds up weather it’s one extra you earn or one less you spend. ) I enjoy the writing tasks the most, as well as taking the surveys. There is just so many different tasks to choose from though I think I’ll always be able to find something to do. Even though the tasks don’t pay enough to be considered a part time job, they don’t take very long to complete either. I always search for HIT’s with the most available, hopefully finding something enjoyable to do. Then I search for hits that pay the most per task. You just have to develop your own style based on the type of hit‘s that you like and what you are after. Fun or money, or just spending a few hours turking. Hopefully lots more people and businesses will discover the great labor pool and become requesters on Mechanical Turk. Weather they do or not I’ll be here completing hit’s and having a blast
