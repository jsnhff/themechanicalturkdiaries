Being a busy physiotherapist, I never had any time to spare a year ago, but then things changed when I had to migrate just for my partner, and had enough time to spare during weekdays till my partner returned from work. After I heard from one of my IT friends about turking, I got interested to it and found mechanical turk. I found it interesting, because it dint have anything much to do with IT.I love writing and article writing about unusual topics interested me. From then on I used to spend a couple of hours after my work whenever I get time. I never mind the reward amount usually, as far as the article is interesting so that I can learn new things, but was surprised to find it was enough for me to spend few dollars extra while shopping for groceries every week. Though not a big deal, I love it because it gives me a sense of satisfaction, especially for the writer part of me. When I said about this to my partner, she would not believe and was surprised ,of course she did not know much about the writer part of me and when I told her, she voluntarily helps me in editing few things if I don’t have time. Turking has even helped me to better my skills in writing, analysing and expressing it in the right way. The articles which I have contributed so far, ranges from repairing a pen to a research article about osteoporosis. In short, it is different, it is amazing and it is worth a try.
