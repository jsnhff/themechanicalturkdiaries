--- title: "At age 12 I began to doubt my faith" date: 2009-06-22 04:31:05 +0000 tumblr_id: "127896054" notes: 0 shown: "Jun. 22 2009" month: "2009-06" permalink: /post/127896054/ ---

I was fortunate enough to grow up in a Christian home with parents, grandparents and extended relatives who modeled what it meant to be a believer. At age 5, I accepted Christ into my heart. But at age 12 I began to doubt my faith. I had reached that point where my faith had to become my own. I wondered whether or not God was real, if there really was an eternity, etc. I cried out to God asking for Him to prove Himself and He did! Since then I have never looked back. I got baptized in water and the Spirit that year and while I am so far from perfect and have made some bad choices along the way, my prayer is that my life will reflect the light and love of my Savior!
