I guess you could say that I’m obsessed with Mechanical Turk. I first found out about Mechanical Turk on msn.com. It sounded like the perfect hobby for me. I love to make extra money. I have a full-time job; but I like to do other things to make extra money. In addition to mechanical turk; I also sell things at flea markets and sell things on Ebay. I enjoy mechanical turk because I can perform tasks while watching televesion. I also sometimes work on hits during my lunch hour. You don’t make much money; but it’s a fun hobby. I especially enjoy the surveys. I feel like I am having a say in the world and that my voice is being heard. I also enjoy the editing hits. One of the additional reasons I enjoy mechanical turk is because I like to watch television on weeknights to relax after a long day of work. Working on mechanical turk while watching tv makes it seem like I’m not wasting my time. It’s fun and relaxing and you can work on it at your own pace. It also makes the commercials go by faster. I don’t know if I will continue to work on mechanical turk hits forever. At this point I don’t have any desire to stop. It’s fun and easy; and it keeps my mind sharp. I don’t make enough money to make that much of an impact on my life. I usually make around $39 every few weeks. But that’s enough to pay for a night out or a nice meal a few times a month.
