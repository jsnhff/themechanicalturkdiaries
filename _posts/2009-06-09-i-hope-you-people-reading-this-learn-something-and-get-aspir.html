Hi I am mechanical student in famous university in Kerala , India . One our teacher came to our class and told about a science exibition in our city and all major universities are coming . So we were allow to participate in that and a topic was to be selected . So after along debate we decide to work on green earth and decided to make an electric car , with the help of our teacher we completed the project . Even though we got only 2nd prize many of the jury came and said it was the most innovative idea and good for nature we were happy that we could do something for nature . Then onwards our university in honour if our nature decided to organize green fest and many other universities were also invited for that . Then onwards evry year this green fest is being on and we students who won the prize is allowed to organize this programe . It is a great honour for us students even though we left university . I hope you people reading this learn something and get aspiration from this and do something for mother nature.
