Born in the northwest, I was always an independent, yet loving child. I enjoy bead work and crafting and always have. I paint on the weekends when I have the energy. I have one beautiful school age child and another on the way. I fight to have these children because I suffer with Hyperemesis during pregnancy. They become not just precious treasures but hard won precious treasures. My family is my life. I don’t have many friends as I want to spend all my extra time with my family. Some may say I have too many pets. “What’s too many?” I say. I have always enjoyed cars and all they entail. They are both a hobby and a passion. I have lusted after them since I was a young child. Someday I may be able to afford the most sought after ones. I’m spiritually joined with a sexy mechanic. We have lusty nights and fun days together hence the pregnancy. We don’t need a slip of paper to prove love and devotion, it just exists. We are well on the way to buying a new home. Our future has never been clearer. Maybe it should be a farm, with all the pets and all. Our path is clear, our future is bright. I believe in us, in our family. All families should have the pure love ours has. Thanks.
