I have always been a bit of an insomniac. So I usually spend many a late nights or early mornings just wasting time sitting at my computer. I mostly click away and “stalk” people on social networking sites or I will visit all my favorite store’s websites to wishfully view the many beautiful clothes that I can’t afford. But now, on those many nights I can’t sleep or days I don’t have to work, I can sit in the comfort of my own home and use my computer time to make money! I always jump at any chance at all to make a bit of extra cash, so naturally I was super excited when I found out about this Mechanical Turk. It really passes the time, and it is almost like a game to see how much money I can make. I absolutely love it when I log in and my total earned has grown! I can’t wait for the day I have “turked” enough to actually withdraw the money I have spent so much time accumulating. I have also always been a strong believer in that I need to work puzzles such as sudoku or something similar to keep my mind strong, active, and healthy. I feel like some of the HITs will do that for me. Rewriting sentences or categorizing things keeps me thinking over the summer, when my mind would otherwise be going dead! As I slave away at my computer into the wee hours of the night I just keep in mind that even though it is just change I am making at the time, it will add up!
