I may very well be one of the busiest people I know that doesn’t have children. I work part time at a grocery store, run my own business that has been going pretty well (just not well enough for me to quit the grocery store gig). I like to pretend that I am an amazing wife, I try to keep our house clean and my husband fed healthy meals, we just found out he is having some health problems and then at the end of the day I try to sit down for few hours to Turk so I can feed my amazon addiction! I am an avid animal lover. I have 6 cats and a dog (2 of the cats aren’t even mine ) and I help rescue animals that are in need of a good home or are in danger of being put to sleep. My days off are spend taking care of my 80 year old grandmother and on top of all of this I am studying Political Science at the local University. And that is just a little about me!
