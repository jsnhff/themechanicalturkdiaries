I joined Mechanical Turk two weeks back and have already earned a good amount of money. I had to buy a gift for my boy friend which was too costly and I needed enough money for that in three months time. So, I had begun a search for online jobs. At first I got engaged with a few survey websites but they were not really frequent with the surveys and paid less than what I wanted. Then I came across a blog which had mentioned Mechanical Turk as one of the options for online jobs. I immediately joined it and found it to be the best place for earning online. The tasks were so interesting. I got some really good paying surveys on the site. In addition to that, there were many fun and creative tasks as well. I even wrote a lot many articles for people. Frequency of hits wasn’t a problem at all. I can choose my time of working and the tasks I want to complete. It’s just two weeks and I’m already addicted to the site.
