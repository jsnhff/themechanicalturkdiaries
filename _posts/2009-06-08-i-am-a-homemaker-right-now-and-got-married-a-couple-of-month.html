I am a homemaker right now and got married a couple of months back. I was working before my marriage. But now my husband is not ready to send me to job. So right now happily leading my life as a homemaker. To spend time properly and usefully started working on mturk. I have handful of interesting experiences when I was working. I was working for almost 5 years and changed 5 companies. I started my career as a project trainee for a stipend of very meager amount. But struggled hard and worked sincerely. I was a software engineer infact. So all my time gone into computers and coding. Will always be thinking of how to achieve an optimum solution. My hometown was actually very far from my office so I was staying in working women hostel.Will have nice fun and enjoyment during weekends. My parents was getting worried as I was growing older and it’s high time that I should get married. Started searching a guy for me. But I had more constraints for my partner due to which it became difficult for my parents to find even a single guy matching my constraints. I too took steps by registering in matrimonial sites. To my fortune I found my partner in one of the site and got introduced myself through chat and mails. We both got a mutual understanding and were ready to get married. So we shared our feelings for each other to our parents. Both of our parents immediately agreed and finally got married.
