Hi; my name is Fran and I live in New England. I’m married, 55 years old, and we have a 20 year old daughter. I grew up in new york city in a middle class neighborhood. i have 2 younger sisters and believe me when i say the family was dysfunctional. my parents would argue quite often and it was a childhood with a lot of stress. I was a mediocre student; the only subject I constantly did well in (besides lunch) was english. My current family is much different than the family i grew up with. i have a real nice wife, my daughter is great although quite unpredictable sometimes. I have a very boring job and it doesn’t pay much. At 55 years old, i’m nervous that i’ll be laid off some day and at my age it will be hard to find another job, especially in the part of new england where i am located. it’s a pretty small town, there isn’t a major city near by and it would be quite hard to find something else. the money i make on M-Turk really comes in handy. Thanks and best wishes.
