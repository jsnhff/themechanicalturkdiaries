First of I thank you for giving chance to share my feelings with all my Mturk friends. I joined in Mechanical Turk one and half year back. Starting I got very confused to do the work. Later I got the work done well. After getting the money I brought one shirt to my husband and toy kit to my kid. They felt very happy.
Later I got confidence that I can do something to my family. Recently we took a house of our own. For that purpose we took gold loan for initial payment. So now I am struggling hard to recover that gold loan so that I could help my husband from my way. My son saw a XBOX in my friend’s house. He stood on his legs that he wants that XBOX. As it is very expensive, I trying a lot to buy that for him. To be frank Mechanical Turk is helping me a lot. Now a day’s day and night I am trying my best to earn more money for mu kid. It is very hard for me to look after my kid, house hold things, my teaching profession and Mturk.
But to fulfill my son’s wished I should work hard. Otherwise he will get very upset which I can’t see. So I am thanking the Mturk for giving this kind of chance where we can earn at any time, instead of simply sitting at home and fulfill our family member’s requirements. When our family is happy more we don’t need anything. Even I giving 5% of my earning to homeless people which makes me feel satisfied.
