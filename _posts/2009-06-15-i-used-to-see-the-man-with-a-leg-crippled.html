--- title: "I used to see the man with a leg crippled" date: 2009-06-16 00:09:00 +0000 tumblr_id: "124245312" notes: 0 shown: "Jun. 15 2009" month: "2009-06" permalink: /post/124245312/ ---

I used to see the man with a leg crippled begging for alms.He is mentally not very sound.So he would call out every one standing in the bus stand for a cup of coffee.Daily he used to beg for coffee from me.I also took pity on that man and offered him money to get coffee.I did it often though not regularly. The out break of chickencunia,a dangerous epidemic completely crippled the normal life in the town.So this begger was also a victim lying near a dust bin uncared, begging for treatment.Nobody including myself offered help.But a good hearted police took him to near by government hospital for treatment!I dare not go that side because my heart would not tolerate the pathetic sight.After a week I happened to pass that side noticed unwillingly the place where he was lying.I saw the place empty and a vender who knew me told that the man succumbed to death due to the epidemic inspite of the treatment offered freely.Only a local governing officers made arrangement for the disposel of the body,as he was a lonely man lived by himself.
