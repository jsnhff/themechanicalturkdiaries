Iam a girl name reshma belong to an aristocratic family. I was born and brought up in a serene locality near kerala. At the age of sixteen I had an affair with a boy and we shared each other and spent a lot of time together. Unfortunately he belong to a poor and low caste family. So my parents did not permit me to marry him.

At the age of twenty I got married to a person who belongs to our caste and creed. He was rich, well educated and good at heart. I told him about the affair I had in my teenage. He supported and loved me and our life was very happy.

After 5 years I met the boy whom I loved in my teenage and I was shocked to know that he did not marry any girl as he loved me sincerely.He is still living as single and this makes my heart wound and sorrow.
