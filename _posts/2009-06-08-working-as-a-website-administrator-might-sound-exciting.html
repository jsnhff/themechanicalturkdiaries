Working as a website administrator might sound exciting, but it can get very boring doing the same thing over again, which is why I was looking for something to distract me. And working for Mechanical Turk is a great one. I used to just sit and play Scrabble online and at least now, I am getting paid. I started about two weeks ago and I’m up to around $30, which doesn’t seem like much but I’m sure it will keep adding up the more I do. It’s nice to look at it as some extra spending money that hopefully will go towards me moving within the next few months and being able to buy some household things. I love doing the polls the most! The reason I was attracted to Mechanical Turk us because Amazon is behind it and I trust them. There are so many online jobs out there that can be pretty sketchy and pay you every month through check. I love MTurk because you can get paid whenever you want and have the funds go directly to your paypal account! I trust this site and think its great. The only thing I find crazy is when very low pay is offered for an assignment that can be complex. I usually just skip over those and go to the ones that are worth my time. I am probably going to be working in my spare time for MTurk for as long as it is available since it really is a great way to making a little extra money on my down time, especially when I am already getting paid for my main job!
