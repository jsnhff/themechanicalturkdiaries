I am a Mechanical Turker. Admittedly, I was a little skeptical about making money on the Internet; one can be so easily scammed these days, but when a trusted friend told me about the site I decided to check it out. This is such a great way to make a little bit of extra money in one’s spare time. I have been able to make enough money to buy some of my textbooks already, and I am looking forward to being able to supplement my income to allow me to go out to eat more or just have fun. Some skill-building is involved in the process, which I enjoy. Instead of being mind-numbingly boring work, I feel like I am building up some skills that I didn’t have before such as being able to creatively answer a question in a short amount of time. I find that I am able to weed out the information that I do not need from the information that I do need a little faster since beginning this. Also, I can use all the free time I was wasting before to put a little money in my pocket. I’ll admit that I haven’t told many people about it. Being a little selfish I don’t want all the good hits to be taken up so fast! Any of my friends, though, that I think would benefit from the extra money I tell. I’m not completely selfish.
