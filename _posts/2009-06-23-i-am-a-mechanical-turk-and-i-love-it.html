I needed some extra money to help pay for my Amazon purchases, so I decided to search the internet for way to make money. On someone’s website, I found a link to mTurk. I joined it and instantly fell in love with it. All of the hits on mTurk are very easy to do. Some take a lot of time and some take very little time. I was able to purchase my USB memory card reader using money I’ve made on Mechanical Turk. The money I made to help pay for it was the easiest money I’ve ever earned. At a couple of discussion boards that I am a member of, I’ve heard from other people how they luck mTurk too! There are so many web sites out there that just turn out to be scams. It is so great that mTurk is NOT a scam! Every day, I do hits and earn money. They money just keeps adding up. I already know what I want to buy next with the money I’m earning. The fact that I can use this money to pay for Amazon is greater than great. I am an avid Amazon shopper and love when the items are purchased for little to nothing. I had my boyfriend sign up for it too, and he really likes it as well. I plan on spreading the good news about Amazon Mechanical Turk to everyone and anyone who wants to earn a little money. I am a Mechanical Turk and I LOVE it!
