My family. It is large and blended in many areas and we all love each other dearly. No one in this family has step parents or step/half siblings. When the blended families came in, they were accepted wholly. In one case it meant that I became a great grandmother at the age of 52 but so be it. That great grand puts a sparkle in my eye as quick as any of the rest and I love her for it. I once had a dream of taking off from everything and running off to the Yukon. I still have it tucked back and dream about it occassionally but it will never happen. Unconditional love, trust, companionship and security go a long way in my world and I wouldn’t give it up for anything.
