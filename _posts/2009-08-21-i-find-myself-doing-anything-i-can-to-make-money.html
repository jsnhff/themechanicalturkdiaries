Not only are employees at companies around the world being affected by the current economic crisis, but so are graduate students! I’m a graduate student at a prestigious University, who lost his stipend money when the project he was working on was cut. I have to finish the project in order to get my degree, but I no longer have any sort of income! Thus, I find myself doing anything I can to make money, be it doing these HITs online or psychology studies or medical studies. I hope the economy improves soon, so that other graduate students aren’t forced to deal with the same hardships I have been forced to overcome to get my degree.
