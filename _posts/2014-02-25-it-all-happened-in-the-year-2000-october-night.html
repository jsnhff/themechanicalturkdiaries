It all happened in the year 2000 October night . I was suffering from severe chicken pox with all over the scars on my body with high temperature and body pain. My room was filled the smell of rotten fruits and I too felt very disturbed. My mum asked whether I want a bath and I agreed with her. That evening I took a bath and the night was very terrible that I started crying of pain. My eyes were also filled with the poxes and it made to blind to see. I was crying calling mother and in the middle of the night I started feeling thirsty and was not able to detect where the water was ,so I called mum and she came in till that time there was heavy rain with thunderstorm. And when my mum came in the nature was peace but the power supply had gone. My mum cursed the electricity people because there was no light to take medicine and water for me and she was also panic seeing my situation. Suddenly a blushing green light started to appear within my door ventilator and my room was full of light my mum gave me the medicine and water and laid me on to bed and then the light disappeared and mum said to me papa has awaken and showing his torch light from the ventilator. Next day when my mum told my papa that I was so sick last night and his torch light helped to give medicine my papa was stunned that he was so sleepy that he never even knew what had happened last night. My sister who was sleeping in the room told that she cuddled her head into the blankets of the bright light. Any way from that day on wards my scars stated to heel , my fever and body aches left and I got cured suddenly. So its really a mystery till now but I believe that it was the rays from the statue of the our lady of health which was kept on the ventilator of the adjacent room because that statue always brightens in the same color during night .To say till now In every situation that made me confused was saved by my mother of health and I am sure she is always besides me to see, hear and protect me and my family in every step we take. I have felt her invisible hands being covering me when the times I steps into the the wrong path, when my eyes are with tears due to mental pains all the time I feel her blessings with my husband, my children ,my parents, my siblings and all who believes in her.
