I am a stay at home mom trying to keep a family together, contribute financially to them and the economy and stay sane in these tough times! I am not anything special, just a regular person trying to contribute, raise happy good children and have people remember me fondly. It’s been said that everyone brings joy, some when they enter a room and others when they leave, I want to be the one bringing joy when I enter not when I leave. I am the daughter of teachers, big sister of two brothers, mom to three little girls and wife to a great guy. We live quiet lives in the suburbs of Cleveland, vote (big democrats), contribute to charities and attend church. My husband is Catholic, I am Protestant but really more spiritual than anything else. I love dogs, kids, traveling, learning and being happy. I want to do what I can to make the world kinder, safer and more peaceful for my family and human beings everywhere. We all count and should stand together to accoomplish this!
