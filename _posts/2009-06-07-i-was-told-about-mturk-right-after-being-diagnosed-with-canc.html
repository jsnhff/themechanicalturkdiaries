I was told about MTurk right after being diagnosed with cancer. I was sitting at home after my first surgery bored as could be and scared as could be. My husband heard about this site and thought that doing the hits would keep my mind off of what was happening and make me feel like I was doing something. He was right. This site helped me keep my sanity during a very hard time and helped me to stay busy. Thanks MTurk
