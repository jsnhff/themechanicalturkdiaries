I currently just graduated college with a music education degree. I am relocating to another country in a month to begin a teaching job. I will be teaching the natives of the country English at a small private school. Although I love music, this job will give me a much needed break from the activity. Like many recent graduates, I am currently in financial turmoil. I am looking for any way possible to make money. I have done work for a few employers but unfortunately the money has not come through yet. Although they are reputable institutions, the paperwork is traveling slow. I would pick up another part time job but no one will hire someone for a month and I understand that. I am slightly worried because it does cost a lot of money to start up in a new location, money that I do not currently have. I do have faith that the money from my previous employers will come through but I am scrambling right now for any money I can get. I stumbled across Mechanical Turk when searching how to get quick money. Although the return at the moment is small, every penny counts in my current situation. Once I start my new teaching job in a month, I will be fine. Making it through this month will be the first challenge. I am finding despite the small return, I do enjoy the small tasks that it requires. I am almost becoming borderline obsessed with it. Although there are other things I could be doing, I am enjoying my time as a mechanical turk.
