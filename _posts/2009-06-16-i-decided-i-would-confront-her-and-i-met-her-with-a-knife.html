So for the last three nights I have had the most vivid, confusing dreams, they’ve actually led me to consider buying one if those silly dream dictionaries. I think I’ve probably been having them because I’m going through the home buying process right now. A few of the main points though… I had this little baby bird that kept following me. It wasn’t some beautiful exotic bird, just a little robbin like creature. But everytime I saw anyone they would try and step on the little bird! Especially one person who began chasing me to get to the bird. It was weird, weird weird. I drove off in to the mountains to try and be alone. When I say alone I mean with Bailey, and the bird. (No Ian???) Now, this one particular person was still looking for me, I kept getting this ominous feeling that she was going to kill my little bird. I kept seeing her driving looking for us all. She was so angry. We kept moving to keep away. Then, I decided I would confront her, and I met her with a knife?!? She tried again to kill the bird. I put it at her throat and yelled at her (O.o;;;) to leave us all alone. She wouldn’t stop trying to hurt the bird so I did something terrible. O.o And as she laid there all I felt was relief. Weird.
