I’m proud to say that I am a turker. Currently, I’m a college student who is unemployed. Most of my schooling is covered by scholarships, however, I still need some money to go out with my friends. Using mturk, I’m managed to make some ends meet and pay for a meal or two. With a little luck, I’ll have enough for a date with the girlfriend tomorrow! It’s not easy, but there is money for work, even if it is menial.
