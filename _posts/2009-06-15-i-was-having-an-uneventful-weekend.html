I was having an uneventful weekend and I was going to go into work with my roomate since we both work in the same place and had the same idea. When we arrived at the building we realized we didn’t have our access badges, and thus we couldn’t get in, and had to walk back to our apartments. As it was a warm day once we got our badges we also changed our shirts and set out to go back to work. We were half way back when my roommate realized he had put his badge into the shirt he had then taken off, and we went back to the apartment to get it, again. In the end we got into the building and got some work done - the irony being that the weekend this ended up not being an entirely uneventful weekend.
