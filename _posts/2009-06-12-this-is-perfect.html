I choose to do Mechanical Turks after one of my husband’s coworkers jokingly told him about it as a job for me because they had heard about it on a pod cast. When I checked it out the first time I thought it was completely worthless and a waste of time until I learned how to use it correctly. I thought it was kind of neat to be able to sit down at night after all my kids have gone to sleep and make some extra cash while I watch TV. I am a stay at home mom and the little bit of extra cash we just use for little fun things for the kids (like outings or little grab bag toys). I love making money and not having to work a set amount of hours or go into an office. I love being able to do it from the comfort of my home and in my pjs. I also love not having to drive a car or go anywhere. I have worked several stay at home jobs over the last 7 years and they have always been hard to do while trying to raise three babies. This is perfect for me because I never have to worry about my kids being to loud or my employer being upset about the amount of time it takes me to complete tasks. I always tell people about it so that they can make a little extra cash in these hard economic times too.
