I believe it was exactly 48 days ago, around night-time when I first stumbled across this site, Mturk. I was working on clixsense.com where you click ads to get paid and from that site’s forums, I came across Mturk. At first, I did not do any hits immediately. After some days, I caught sight of this wonderful top on Amazon and since it was a little too pricey for me, I migrated back to Mturk.

The free time in which I would have done something less than productive like reading some magazines and stuff are now devoted to Mturk. I would say basically 2-3 hours per day with exceptions like holidays.

I love working through Mturk. I don’t like to wait if I want to do some work. Some sites that I frequented offered more privileges if you spent some money on that site and more work would be available. Mturk is not like that. I don’t like having to wait for days while the requester makes his selection. If I have free time and I want to work, I want to work right now.

Working through hits is great in my opinion. As I said before, I like to do work when I feel like. Not having to wait to do work is a huge plus point for me. HITS are easy, swift and uncomplicated and competitive. After all, everyone likes a little bit of that race in their life.

The payments would definitely be better. Some requesters pay dirt-cheap prices but I guess one can just avoid those HITs like I do. I believe that we all should be paid very fairly for our work because we, those who do work hard, try to put our best into it.
