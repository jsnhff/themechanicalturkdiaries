I went on a picnic to Satapada with my friends. Though we had planned to explore the beaches but due to paucity of time we had to change that decision and decided to spend our picnic at the forested areas of Satapada. I did not find the area fruitful still i felt it was better than cancelling the picnic. We reached Satapada on time and while our food was being cooked, we all decided to explore the forest life. I was a bit scared because there were huge trees all around and i knew that if it gets late then it would be get dark and might create a hindrance for us to get out of the forest. So i asked all my friends to move together so that everyone stays together. While everyone was busy taking photographs, i was constantly looking at my watch out of fear. I felt as though a tiger or a lion would attack me from the back. Suddenly i saw a beautiful plant with yellow flowers that caught my attention and i kept watching it for minutes. After few minutes i observed that no one was around. I got panicked and went mad. I shouted but could not hear anyone’s voice. I kept running here and there but traced no one. I was angry on my friends because they did not make any efforts to wait for me. I had no battery in my cell phone as well. Suddenly i heard few weird noises and i was so terrified that i started running helplessly. Thankfully, i saw a bus standing at one corner and that was the one that we all had come to Satapada. I asked the driver to drop me at the picnic spot where i saw my friends enjoying and partying. I was hugely upset but then i did not want to spoil their mood. So i decided not to narrate my harrowing story to them and rather enjoy with them.
