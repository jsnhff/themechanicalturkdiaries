I’ve lost my home, I lived out of my car. I’ve lost my car, I’m living on the streets. I have a storage, I had a job that pays cash. Now I’m working on getting my bank account out of the negative due to bank fraud before I lose my storage. I’ve found a lot of online job offers, most of them require you to pay them upfront which is really suspicious anyway you look at it. Then I stumbled on some legitimate jobs that not only are free, but it is easy to sign up for. Two of them that say they pay, I guess they pay, but its very tricky. Before that I was reading paid emails, which pay you extremely low. It would take you a whole day to make a penny on those sites. I’ve been getting kicked out of stores for the very fact that I’m homeless, I’ve had cops called on me many times. I’ve met with some cool friends that are in my same situation. We talk about our experiences. I tell you it is hectic being homeless. Be careful where you sleep. You never know what you might wake up into. There are scavengers, and bum bashers out there. I would’ve gone to a shelter but unfortunately, from what I’ve read and heard, they can be dangerous. Right now I have a hiding place where I go to, I lie down there and pray no one finds me until I’m able to get a place to stay. I have some people helping me out, but I’m doing the best I can to help myself so I don’t become a burden on them. I’m not taking it as bad as I thought I would, but I do miss playing with my electronics. I have DVD players, PS2, computer and 6 channel stereo in my storage. I’m looking forward to being able to play those again.
