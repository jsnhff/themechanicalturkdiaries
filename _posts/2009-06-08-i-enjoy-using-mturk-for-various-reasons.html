I enjoy using Mturk for various reasons. Many of the hits are fun to do, and a way to make even just a little extra cash when needed. I have been struggling with my weight all my life, and I was very pleasantly surprised to come across a hit in which you test a weight loss program. It was nice to find weight loss help where I didn’t have to spend my own money on something that may, or may not work. So I’m very excited to see how that pans out. In addition, I enjoy being able to help others, even if it’s just writing a simple review for something. I feel like I’m contributing something to society, which is a good feeling. Some of the websites I have found while doing hits, are really interesting, and I probably would never have known about them otherwise. At first I was a little skeptical, a lot of online money makers are more of a headache than anything. But I’ve been very pleased with Mturk so far. I have a lot of fun doing the hits and am learning new things as well. I haven’t made much money yet, but due to other things going on in my life, I haven’t been able to devote a whole lot of time to Mturk until just recently. I can’t wait to see what new hits will be put up over the next few weeks!
