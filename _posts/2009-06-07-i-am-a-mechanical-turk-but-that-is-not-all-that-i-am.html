I am a Mechanical Turk. But that is not all that I am. I am also a homeowner and a dog owner. Last summer my crazy beagle was chasing a rabbit around the back yard. We have a pool with a chain-link fence around it and the rabbit managed to squeeze under that fence and hide under the decking surrounding the pool. My dog (I’ll call her Daisy) quickly dug under the fence also and then proceeded to sqeeze under the deck after the rabbit. Daisy got about 12 feet under the deck and then got stuck! We could hear her, but could not see her. We hoped she would be able to somehow wiggle her way out backwards but she could not. Then it started to pour rain and thunder and lightening. We didn’t want to leave her out there so my son got a crow bar and pulled up several of the boards of the deck (ruining them) so Daisy could get out. She acted as if nothing was wrong and followed us into the house…where she go a bath. I would like to think that Daisy is a smart dog, but the same exact thing happened the next day. This time there was no rain or thunder or lightening so we took our time pulling up the boards so that they could be nailed back into place. We realized this was going to be an on going event if we didn’t do something about it. So my husband and son had to go around the whole fence hammering in stakes so Daisy could no longer dig under it. It worked and now the rabbits are safe as well as our deck.
