I am just a girl. I grew up in very humble surroundings in a tiny little town. I was protected from everything imaginable by my Mother. Although she kept me sheltered from the world, I still managed to experience everything that you would never want to have happen to your daughter. I attempted suicide when I was 14. I was raped when I was 15 by an adult on our family. I had my first child when I was 17. I have had a scary stalker. I have been held at hostage at gun-point. I was an abused wife. I managed to escape all of this and now I am in a very happy marriage, and I am a successful business owner. If you ever think that you can not overcome challenges, just think of me… I am just a girl.
