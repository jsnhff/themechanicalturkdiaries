I do this quite casually and have been able to stack up a few extra bucks. I don’t think that I will ever earn a living with this but I have been able to save some money on Amazon.com. Most recently I purchased a new camera and saved around fifty dollars by using money I accumulated turking. All in all its not a bad way to fill some time and make a couple of bucks. I have been work at getting some higher qualifications so i can work on the big money HITS. I never would have guessed that I would be doing anything like this. I am still searching for that get rich quick scheme so if you got any ideas send them my way. Thanks for reading and keep on turking baby!
