I was born in 1985 in augusta ga. I cant recall almost nothing of the first four years of my life. It could be said that I had a happy childhood that included a brother and four sisters. Based on my report cards, I was an average student. My father was a teacher and my mother was a housewife. We lived in a small town called Augusta and were active in the baptist Church. At the age of ten I joined the Boy Scouts of America. During high school I was active in sports. At the age of sixteen I took up deer hunting and also began dating. After high school I attended college for 2 years. After graduating from college I got married and worked as a life insurance salesman. Four years after graduating from college I landed a secure job as a land acquisition agent. Within the first year of my gainful employment we bought a cozy brick house and we had a bouncing baby boy and one year later a beautiful daughter. Within the third year of my employment we had another baby girl. In 1993 we bought land and moved into a country home. Much of my life has been devoted to working and raising a family. My son attained the rank of Eagle Scout in 2002. My daughter graduated with honors in 2006. Both are presently enrolled in college and now my spouse works as a Registered nursi in a wonderful hospital. Much of my leisure time has been spent outdoors, hunting, fishing or hiking in the mountains.
