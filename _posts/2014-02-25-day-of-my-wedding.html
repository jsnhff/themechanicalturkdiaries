Day of my wedding

My wedding was on Oct 21 2013. I woke that morning at my apartment and felt great and excited to be getting married. I hung around my apartment to about 1:00pm and then went to pick up my tux. After I got my tux I went to the hotel where we were having the wedding. It is a beautiful old hotel that was designed like a mission with Spanish influence. When I got to the hotel I went up to our suite and met up with my groomsman. We all got dressed together and had a couple of drinks. Then we took a few pictures with the photographer and headed down to the chapel. I was a little nervous but all the butterflies went away when I saw my beautiful wife walking down the aisle. She looked amazing and she just blew me away. The ceremony went smoothly and after that we took some more pictures throughout the hotel. After pictures we headed down to the reception hall and did our grand entrance. Next up was the best man and maid of honor speeches, the speeches were heartfelt and everyone loved them. We did a dollar dance as well and collect a bunch of money to use on the honeymoon. After we did the money dance we cut the cake. The cake was a beautiful ivory three tier cake with red velvet filling. When we were done with the cake we dance for hours at the reception. When only had the reception room till 12:00am so after that we headed to the bar and spent time with our family and friends. The wedding was amazing overall and I couldn’t be happier.
