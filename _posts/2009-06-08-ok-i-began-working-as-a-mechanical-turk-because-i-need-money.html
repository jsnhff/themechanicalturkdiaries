Ok, I began working as a Mechanical Turk because I need money! After researching what “Mechanical Turk” is, I found out that it is a legit business which is so rare to find nowadays and I would get paid right away. The problem is, the pay is not that great! To be honest, I have a wedding this August, behind on bills, and was lowered my workload due to the fabulous economy. Talk about bad timing. So to sum it up, I am willing to be a Mechanical Turk Slave and hope to find as many one or two dollar hits as possible! I know this is not possible because I see hundreds of hits paying one cent to fifty cents but hey, it will add up. I am only able to work from home due to my special needs son who needs me here so I only have the internet as my work outlet and finding any kind of work out there is very important to me right now. I will bundle up as many hits as possible and get them done as soon as possible because it is income for me and my family. It is not much at all but it is something, if not, some grocery money for the week or a bill or two to get out of the way. I am just another American trying to survive and keep up with life that will keep going! I really hope to see more better paying hits available!
